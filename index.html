About Annona

What is Annona?

A smart platform designed to make group work fair, organized, and transparent.

Fair Contribution Tracking

Annona uses a smart scoring algorithm to measure each team member's real contribution — based on tasks completed, hours logged, and activity.

Built for Students & Teams

Whether it's a university project or a startup team, Annona ensures everyone's effort is visible, reducing conflicts and boosting accountability.

Data-Driven Insights

Visual analytics and leaderboards give you a clear picture of team performance, helping you identify strengths and areas for improvement.

Features

Everything You Need to Manage Your Team

Powerful tools to organize tasks, track contributions, and analyze team performance.

Task Management

Organize work with a Kanban-style board. Create, assign, and move tasks through To Do, In Progress, and Done columns.

Contribution Tracking

See exactly who did what. Every task completion, update, and action is tracked and attributed to the right team member.

Smart Contribution Score

Our algorithm calculates a fair score based on tasks completed, hours logged, and activity — no more guessing who contributed most.

Team Collaboration

Invite team members, assign roles, and work together seamlessly. Everyone stays on the same page with real-time updates.

Analytics Dashboard

Visualize team performance with interactive charts — pie charts for contribution breakdown and bar charts for task completion.

Activity Logs

A complete timeline of all team actions — task creations, completions, and status updates with timestamps.
